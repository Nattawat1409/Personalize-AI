# Safety Guidelines

**Department:** Laboratory
**Scope:** Chemical handling and analytical instruments

## Chemical handling

- Hydrofluoric acid for XRF prep: fume hood only, calcium gluconate gel on hand
- Store concentrated acids in the ventilated corrosives cabinet, never above
  eye level
- Segregate oxidisers from organic solvents

## Fume hood requirements

Verify face velocity of 0.5 m/s before starting any acid digestion. Do not
store apparatus inside the hood; it disrupts airflow.

## Instrument safety

- XRF: interlock must be verified weekly; never bypass the sample chamber switch
- Muffle furnace: tongs and face shield above 500°C, no plastic near the door

## Spill response

Acid spill under 100 mL: neutralise with sodium bicarbonate, then absorb.
Over 100 mL or any HF spill: evacuate the lab and call the EHS duty officer.
