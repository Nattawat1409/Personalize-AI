## Cement bag specification

Multi-wall kraft paper sacks, 2-ply for 50 kg fill weight, valve type.
Minimum drop test: 6 drops from 1.2 m, no rupture.

Moisture barrier layer required for coastal and export shipment.

## Storage

Store paper reels at 50–60% RH. Below 40% RH the sheet embrittles and creases
crack during conversion.
