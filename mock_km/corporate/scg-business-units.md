# Business Unit Overview

**Department:** Corporate

## Structure

The group is organised into three primary business units.

### Cement and Building Materials
Cement, ready-mixed concrete, aggregates, roofing, ceramic tiles, and
sanitary ware. Largest unit by headcount.

### Chemicals
Olefins, polyolefins, PVC and downstream polymer products. Serves packaging,
automotive and construction customers.

### Packaging
Integrated pulp, paper, containerboard and flexible packaging solutions.

## Support functions

Logistics, international trading, and corporate R&D operate across all three
units rather than reporting into any single one.

## Customer segments

Construction contractors, building material retailers, FMCG manufacturers
(packaging), and industrial converters (chemicals).
