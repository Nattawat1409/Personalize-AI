# Cement Mix Spec

**Department:** R&D
**Scope:** Laboratory trial formulations, low-carbon programme

## Purpose

Records experimental binder formulations under evaluation for clinker factor
reduction. **Not approved for production use.**

## Trial formulations

| Trial ID | Clinker % | Limestone filler % | Calcined clay % | 28-day strength (MPa) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LC-11 | 65 | 20 | 15 | 42.1 |
| LC-14 | 55 | 15 | 30 | 39.6 |
| LC-19 | 50 | 20 | 30 | 36.2 |

## Test method

Mortar bars cast per lab SOP, cured in saturated lime water at 23°C.
Compressive strength measured at 3, 7, 28 and 90 days, three specimens per age.

## Observations

- LC-14 reaches acceptable 28-day strength with a 45% clinker reduction
- LC-19 shows extended setting time; retarder dosage needs re-evaluation
- Calcined clay above 30% degrades workability sharply at constant w/b

## Status

LC-14 recommended for pilot-scale trial. LC-19 on hold pending admixture study.
