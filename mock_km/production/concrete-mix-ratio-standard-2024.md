# Concrete Mix Ratio Standard

**Version:** 2024-R3
**Effective:** 2024-11-01
**Status:** CURRENT — supersedes 2020-R1
**Department:** Production

## Standard mix designs

| Grade | Cement : Sand : Aggregate | Max w/c ratio | 28-day target |
|---|---|---|---|
| C20 | 1 : 2 : 4 | 0.58 | 20 MPa |
| C25 | 1 : 1.5 : 3 | 0.52 | 25 MPa |
| C30 | 1 : 1.5 : 3 | 0.48 | 30 MPa |
| C40 | 1 : 1 : 2 | 0.42 | 40 MPa |
| C50 | 1 : 1 : 1.8 | 0.38 | 50 MPa |

## Supplementary cementitious materials

Fly ash permitted up to **35%** cement replacement for C20–C30 (raised from 25%
in 2020-R1). Calcined clay blends permitted up to 30% for C20–C25 following the
2023 low-carbon programme.

Ground granulated blast-furnace slag now permitted up to 50% for marine exposure.

## Curing

Minimum 7 days moist curing. Extend to 14 days when ambient exceeds 35°C
(revised from 10 days).

## Slump

Target 75–100 mm general structural. Superplasticiser required for all
w/c below 0.45 — retempering with water remains prohibited.

## Testing

Three cubes per 50 m³ placed, tested at 7 and 28 days. Additional 56-day test
required for any mix using more than 30% SCM replacement.

## Changes from 2020-R1

- All w/c ratio caps tightened
- C50 grade added
- Fly ash limit raised 25% → 35%
- Hot-weather curing extended 10 → 14 days
