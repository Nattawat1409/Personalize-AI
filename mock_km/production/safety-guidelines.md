# Safety Guidelines

**Department:** Plant Operations
**Scope:** Kiln and clinker cooler areas

## Mandatory PPE

- Flame-resistant coveralls in the kiln hood zone
- Face shield plus safety glasses when observing burner flame
- Heat-resistant gloves rated to 250°C for cooler grate work

## Hot work restrictions

- No entry into the kiln shell within 48 hours of shutdown
- Confined space permit required for preheater cyclone entry
- Two-person rule inside the clinker cooler at all times

## Heat stress management

Rotate personnel out of the kiln floor every 30 minutes when ambient exceeds
40°C. Provide electrolyte fluids at the shift station.

## Emergency

Hot meal spill or kiln flush: evacuate to the upwind muster point, do not
attempt cleanup until the area is declared cool by the shift supervisor.
