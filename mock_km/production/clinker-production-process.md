# Clinker Production Process

**Department:** Production

## Raw meal preparation

Limestone and clay are crushed to under 25 mm, proportioned by the online
analyser, and ground in the vertical roller mill to a residue of 12–15% on the
90 µm sieve.

## Pyroprocessing

Raw meal passes through a five-stage preheater tower into the precalciner,
where roughly 90–95% calcination occurs before kiln entry. The rotary kiln
sinters the meal at a burning zone temperature of about 1450°C to form clinker
nodules.

## Kiln control parameters

| Parameter | Normal range |
|---|---|
| Burning zone temperature | 1420–1480°C |
| Kiln speed | 3.2–4.0 rpm |
| Back-end O2 | 2.5–3.5% |
| Free lime in clinker | 0.8–1.5% |

## Cooling

Grate cooler drops clinker from ~1350°C to below 100°C. Rapid cooling preserves
C3S and prevents C2S conversion.

## Common faults

- Rising free lime: check burning zone temperature and raw meal fineness
- Coating loss in burning zone: review flame shape and alkali/sulfur ratio
- Cooler snowman formation: inspect grate air distribution
