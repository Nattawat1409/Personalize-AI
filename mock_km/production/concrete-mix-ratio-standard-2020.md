# Concrete Mix Ratio Standard

**Version:** 2020-R1
**Effective:** 2020-03-01
**Status:** SUPERSEDED by 2024-R3
**Department:** Production

## Standard mix designs

| Grade | Cement : Sand : Aggregate | Max w/c ratio | 28-day target |
|---|---|---|---|
| C20 | 1 : 2 : 4 | 0.60 | 20 MPa |
| C25 | 1 : 1.5 : 3 | 0.55 | 25 MPa |
| C30 | 1 : 1.5 : 3 | 0.50 | 30 MPa |
| C40 | 1 : 1 : 2 | 0.45 | 40 MPa |

## Supplementary cementitious materials

Fly ash permitted up to **25%** cement replacement for C20–C30.
Not permitted for C40 or above.

## Curing

Minimum 7 days moist curing for all grades. Extend to 10 days when ambient
exceeds 35°C.

## Slump

Target 75–100 mm for general structural work. Do not retemper with water on site.

## Testing

Three cubes per 50 m³ placed, tested at 7 and 28 days.
