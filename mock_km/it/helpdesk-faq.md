# IT Helpdesk FAQ

**Department:** IT

## Password reset

Self-service reset via the intranet portal. Passwords expire every 90 days.
Three failed attempts lock the account for 15 minutes.

## VPN access

Request through your line manager. Approval typically takes one business day.
VPN is required for any access to internal systems from outside the office network.

## Software requests

Standard software (Office, browsers, PDF tools) is pre-approved and installs
from the software centre. Anything else requires a ticket with business
justification.

## Reporting an incident

Raise a ticket in the service desk portal. For anything involving suspected
phishing or data loss, call the security hotline immediately rather than
emailing.

## Hardware refresh

Laptops are refreshed on a 4-year cycle. Early replacement requires a fault
report from IT support.
