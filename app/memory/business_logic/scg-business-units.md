## Summary

SCG primarily operates through three main business units: SCG Cement-Building Materials, SCG Chemicals (SCGC), and SCG Packaging (SCGP). The Cement-Building Materials unit focuses on cement, concrete, aggregates, and various building products. SCG Chemicals produces petrochemicals like olefins and polyolefins. SCG Packaging specializes in pulp, paper, and integrated packaging solutions.

- SCG (Siam Cement Group) primarily operates through **three main business units**:

1.  **SCG Cement-Building Materials**: This unit focuses on cement, ready-mixed concrete, aggregates, roof and wall products, floor and surface finishing, sanitary ware, and other building materials.

3.  **SCG Packaging (SCGP)**: This unit specializes in integrated packaging solutions, including pulp, paper, and various types of packaging products.

While they have other supporting businesses and ventures (like SCG Logistics, SCG International, and various digital or innovation-focused initiatives), these three are the core, large-scale business units that define SCG's primary operations.
